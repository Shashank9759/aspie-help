Questions

Quick answers

Do I need a diagnosis to join?

No. Diagnosed, self-identified or still wondering — all welcome. Nobody will ask for proof.

Can other members see my phone number?

In the main Announcements group, only admins can see who the members are. In the topic groups you choose to join, members of that group can see you — so join only the ones you're comfortable with.

Group chats overwhelm me. Is this for me?

Yes. Mute everything, read when you like, join only one group. Many members start that way.

Is this medical help or therapy?

No. It's peer support — people helping people. For a diagnosis, please see a psychiatrist or clinical psychologist.

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free, and it will stay free.
